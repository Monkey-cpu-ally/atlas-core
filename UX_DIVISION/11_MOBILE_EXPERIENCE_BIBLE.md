# ATLAS Mobile Experience Bible

**Version:** 1.0  
**Classification:** Mobile Interface Standard

## Purpose

ATLAS Mobile extends the ecosystem beyond the workstation. It is designed around mobility, speed, awareness, capture, review, and immediate access rather than shrinking the desktop interface onto a phone.

## Mission

Provide a clear, fast, context-aware mobile workspace that keeps users connected to ATLAS projects, knowledge, AI specialists, and supervised systems while away from the desktop.

## Core Principles

1. **Immediate** — important information appears quickly.
2. **Comfortable** — common actions support one-handed use where practical.
3. **Focused** — the current task dominates the screen.
4. **Connected** — projects and conversations synchronize across ATLAS devices.
5. **Context-aware** — presentation adapts to device and environment when appropriate.
6. **Reliable** — graceful degradation and offline access are designed in.

## Primary Navigation

- Home
- Projects
- Knowledge
- AI Core
- Robotics monitoring
- Notifications
- Search
- Settings

Frequently used destinations should normally require no more than two navigation actions.

## Home

The mobile home surface may display:

- Current priorities
- Active projects
- AI conversations
- Research progress
- Connected-system status
- Recent files
- Quick actions

## AI Specialists

Ajani, Minerva, Hermes, and Council retain their established identities. Switching specialists should require one clear interaction.

## Quick Actions

Examples include:

- Start voice interaction
- Call Council
- Create note
- Capture image
- Record idea
- Open current project
- Review important alert

Safety-critical physical-system controls must remain separated from ordinary convenience actions.

## Camera & Capture

The camera may support engineering documentation, whiteboard capture, object/reference capture, visual notes, equipment documentation, and approved scanning workflows.

## Voice

Voice can support questions, navigation, notes, search, and approved commands. Voice is optional and must never be the only way to use essential functions.

## Robotics

Mobile emphasizes monitoring, status, alerts, diagnostics, and deliberate supervisory actions. Full manual control requires an interface and authorization model appropriate to the robot and risk level.

## Offline Mode

Where technically practical, users should retain access to downloaded knowledge, notes, project references, and documentation. Synchronization resumes safely after connectivity returns.

## Battery & Performance

Use adaptive animation, efficient rendering, bounded background work, and low-power presentation modes. Responsiveness takes priority over decorative effects.

## Security

Mobile design should support strong authentication, encrypted local storage, secure synchronization, session management, and protection of sensitive engineering data.

## Accessibility

Support scalable text, sufficient touch targets, keyboard/external input where available, screen readers, reduced motion, high contrast, and non-color-only status communication.

## Final Principle

ATLAS Mobile is a purpose-built extension of the ecosystem: fast enough for immediate decisions, clear enough for field use, and disciplined enough not to pretend a phone is a desktop workstation.
